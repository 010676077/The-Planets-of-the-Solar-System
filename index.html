The Planets of the Solar System

Mercury: The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.

Venus: The hottest planet in the Solar System.

Earth: Our home planet. It has liquid water on its surface.

Mars: Known as the Red Planet.

Jupiter: The largest planet in the Solar System.

Saturn: Famous for its beautiful rings.

Uranus: A cold planet that rotates on its side.

Neptune: The farthest planet from the Sun.
